nothing outside the control plane consumed it. "Consumers verified" states how
absence of live consumers was established before deletion; every entry was
re-verified at deletion time by import/reference search (tracked-file grep plus
the import-block composition-root test in `tests/test_control_plane_free_primitives.py`),
not just from the Task 11 importer map.

control-plane schema migrations (`20260811_03_control_plane.sql` and related)
stay in place so existing databases keep applying cleanly.
